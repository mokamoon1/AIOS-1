# 1. Purpose

This document defines the functional requirements of AIOS.

It describes what the system must do to achieve its investment analysis objectives.

---

# 2. System Objective

AIOS must provide an intelligent investment analysis workflow based on:

* Shariah compliance.
* Market analysis.
* Fundamental analysis.
* Technical analysis.
* Risk management.
* Portfolio management.

---

# 3. Security Universe Management

## FR-001: Shariah Stock Import

The system shall:

* Import Shariah-compliant securities from approved providers.
* Store compliance information.
* Track update dates.

---

## FR-002: Security Filtering

The system shall:

* Block non-compliant securities.
* Reject unknown compliance status.
* Allow only approved securities into analysis.

---

# 4. Market Data Functions

## FR-003: Market Data Collection

The system shall:

Collect:

* Price data.
* Volume data.
* Historical information.

---

## FR-004: Data Storage

The system shall:

* Store collected data.
* Maintain historical records.
* Support future analysis.

---

# 5. Analysis Functions

## FR-005: Market Analysis

The system shall analyze:

* Market trend.
* Market strength.
* Market risk.

---

## FR-006: Fundamental Analysis

The system shall evaluate:

* Company quality.
* Financial strength.
* Growth.
* Valuation.

---

## FR-007: Technical Analysis

The system shall evaluate:

* Price action.
* Market structure.
* Fibonacci.
* SMC concepts.
* Indicators.

---

# 6. Portfolio Functions

## FR-008: Portfolio Construction

The system shall:

* Group investments by sector.
* Calculate allocations.
* Monitor exposure.

---

## FR-009: Portfolio Monitoring

The system shall track:

* Positions.
* Performance.
* Risk exposure.

---

# 7. Decision Functions

## FR-010: Investment Decision Generation

The system shall generate:

* BUY.
* HOLD.
* SELL.
* WAIT.

---

## FR-011: Decision Explanation

Every decision shall include:

* Reason.
* Supporting data.
* Confidence level.
* Risk assessment.

---

# 8. Learning and History

## FR-012: Historical Memory

The system shall store:

* Previous analyses.
* Decisions.
* Performance results.

Purpose:

Improve future evaluations.

---

# 9. Reporting Functions

The system shall provide:

* Analysis reports.
* Portfolio reports.
* Risk reports.
* Decision history.

---

# 10. Automation Functions

The system shall support:

* Scheduled data updates.
* Automated analysis cycles.
* System monitoring.

---

# 11. Functional Limitations

AIOS shall not:

* Make decisions without Shariah verification.
* Execute unauthorized actions.
* Ignore risk controls.
