# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official terminology used throughout the AIOS project.

All developers, AI agents, documentation, and system components must use these definitions.

---

# 2. Project Terms

## AIOS

**Artificial Intelligence Investment Operating System**

The complete investment intelligence platform built using specialized AI agents.

---

## AI_BRAIN

The official knowledge base of the project.

Contains:

- Rules.
- Architecture decisions.
- Requirements.
- Design documents.
- Development standards.

---

## Agent

An independent AI component with a specific responsibility.

Each Agent has:

- Input.
- Processing logic.
- Output.
- Defined permissions.

---

## AI Agent System

A group of specialized agents working together to analyze, evaluate, and manage investment decisions.

---

## CIO Agent

Chief Investment Officer Agent.

The main decision coordination agent.

Responsibilities:

- Collect agent opinions.
- Evaluate recommendations.
- Produce final investment assessment.

---

## Risk Agent

Agent responsible for:

- Risk evaluation.
- Exposure monitoring.
- Position limits.
- Loss prevention.

---

## Portfolio Agent

Agent responsible for:

- Asset allocation.
- Diversification.
- Portfolio optimization.

---

## News Intelligence Agent

Agent responsible for:

- News collection.
- Event analysis.
- Market impact evaluation.

---

# 3. Investment Terms

## Investment Universe

The complete list of assets available for analysis.

In V1:

Only Shariah-compliant stocks.

---

## Shariah Compliance

Verification that a security meets approved Islamic investment criteria.

---

## Shariah Provider

An external trusted source that provides compliance information.

AIOS uses providers as data sources and does not create religious rulings.

---

## Security

A financial instrument that can be analyzed or included in a portfolio.

Example:

Stock.

---

## Stock

An ownership share in a publicly traded company.

---

## Sector

A classification group for companies with similar business activities.

Examples:

- Technology.
- Healthcare.
- Financials.

---

# 4. Market Terms

## Market Data

Financial information used by AIOS.

Examples:

- Prices.
- Volume.
- Historical data.

---

## Historical Data

Previous market records used for:

- Analysis.
- Testing.
- Strategy evaluation.

---

## Technical Analysis

Analysis based on market behavior.

Examples:

- Price.
- Volume.
- Trends.
- Patterns.

---

## Fundamental Analysis

Evaluation of a company based on:

- Financial statements.
- Business quality.
- Performance metrics.

---

## Signal

A structured output indicating a possible investment condition.

Examples:

- Buy candidate.
- Hold.
- Avoid.

---

## Strategy

A defined method used to evaluate investment opportunities.

---

# 5. Trading Terms

## Paper Trading

Simulation of trading without using real money.

Used for:

- Testing.
- Validation.
- Risk evaluation.

---

## Position

The amount invested in a specific asset.

---

## Position Sizing

Determining how much capital should be allocated to an investment.

---

## Portfolio

A collection of investment positions.

---

## Allocation

Distribution of capital among assets or sectors.

---

# 6. System Terms

## Core Engine

The central system responsible for running AIOS services.

---

## Event Bus

A communication system that allows components to exchange information without direct dependency.

---

## Module

An independent software component with a specific responsibility.

---

## API

A communication interface between software systems.

---

## Data Pipeline

The process of collecting, processing, validating, and delivering data.

---

## Database

The storage system used to maintain project information.

---

# 7. Development Terms

## Repository

The Git-managed project storage.

---

## Commit

A saved version of project changes.

---

## Branch

A separate development path in Git.

---

## Pull Request

A request to merge changes after review.

---

## Testing

Process of verifying system correctness.

---

# 8. Decision Terms

## Recommendation

An analysis result produced by the system.

It does not automatically mean execution.

---

## Investment Decision

A final approved action after considering:

- Analysis.
- Risk.
- Portfolio impact.

---

## Explainability

The ability to understand why the system produced a decision.

---

---

## Review

---

## Approved

Document accepted for use.

---

## Locked

Document cannot be changed without formal approval.
