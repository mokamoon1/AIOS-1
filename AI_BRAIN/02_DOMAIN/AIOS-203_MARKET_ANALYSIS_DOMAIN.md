# 1. Purpose

This document defines how AIOS understands and analyzes financial markets.

The purpose is to create a structured market view before evaluating individual investment opportunities.

---

# 2. Market Analysis Philosophy

AIOS does not analyze securities in isolation.

Every investment opportunity must be evaluated within its market context.

The system considers:

* Market condition.
* Sector condition.
* Company condition.
* Individual security behavior.

---

# 3. Market Analysis Hierarchy

AIOS follows a top-down analysis approach:

```text
Global Market Environment

        ↓

Market Index Condition

        ↓

Sector Analysis

        ↓

Company Analysis

        ↓

Security Evaluation
```

---

# 4. Market Environment Analysis

The system evaluates:

## Market Trend

Determines:

* Bullish conditions.
* Bearish conditions.
* Neutral conditions.

---

## Market Strength

Evaluates:

* Breadth.
* Momentum.
* Participation.

---

## Market Risk

Monitors:

* Volatility.
* Sudden movements.
* Market stress.

---

# 5. Sector Analysis

AIOS categorizes approved securities by sector.

Examples:

* Technology.
* Healthcare.
* Industrials.
* Consumer.
* Energy.

The system evaluates:

* Sector performance.
* Sector momentum.
* Relative strength.

---

# 6. Security Analysis Context

Before analyzing a security:

AIOS checks:

```text
Is Market Condition Suitable?

        ↓

Is Sector Strong?

        ↓

Is Security Showing Opportunity?

        ↓

Proceed To Detailed Analysis
```
