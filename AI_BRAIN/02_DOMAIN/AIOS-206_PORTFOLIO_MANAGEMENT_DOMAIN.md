# 1. Purpose

This document defines how AIOS constructs, manages, and evaluates investment portfolios.

The objective is to create diversified portfolios while controlling risk and maintaining Shariah compliance.

---

# 2. Portfolio Philosophy

AIOS does not manage investments by selecting individual securities only.

The system evaluates:

* Portfolio structure.
* Risk distribution.
* Sector exposure.
* Capital allocation.

---

# 3. Portfolio Construction Flow

```text
Approved Securities

        ↓

Security Evaluation

        ↓

Risk Assessment

        ↓

Portfolio Allocation

        ↓

Portfolio Monitoring
```

---

# 4. Portfolio Objectives

The portfolio system aims to achieve:

## Capital Preservation

Protect invested capital through risk controls.

---

## Diversification

Reduce excessive exposure to:

* Single company.
* Single sector.
* Single market condition.

---

## Opportunity Capture

Allocate capital toward attractive opportunities.

---

# 5. Portfolio Components

## Asset Selection

Based on:

* Shariah compliance.
* Fundamental quality.
* Technical opportunity.
* Risk evaluation.

---

## Position Sizing

Determines:

* How much capital is allocated.
* Maximum exposure per security.

---

## Sector Allocation

AIOS categorizes investments by sector.

Examples:

```text
Technology

Healthcare

Industrials

Consumer

Energy
```

---

# 6. Allocation Model

The system evaluates:

```text
Security Score

+

Risk Score

+

Portfolio Impact

=

Allocation Decision
```

---

# 7. Diversification Rules

AIOS must monitor:

## Company Concentration

Avoid excessive allocation to one company.

---

## Sector Concentration

Avoid overexposure to one sector.

---

## Correlation Risk

Avoid holding assets that behave identically.

---

# 8. Portfolio Monitoring

The system tracks:

* Current holdings.
* Performance.
* Risk exposure.
* Allocation changes.

---

# 9. Rebalancing

AIOS evaluates when portfolio adjustment is needed.

Possible reasons:

* Risk increase.
* Sector imbalance.
* Change in investment thesis.
* Shariah status change.

---

# 10. Portfolio Decision Output

The Portfolio Agent produces:

```text
Recommended Allocation

Current Exposure

Risk Level

Diversification Status

Required Actions
```

---

# 11. Integration With Other Agents

## Risk Agent

Provides:

* Risk limits.
* Exposure warnings.

---

## Fundamental Agent

Provides:

* Company quality.

---

## Technical Agent

Provides:

* Timing information.

---

## CIO Agent

Receives portfolio recommendations.

---

# 12. Portfolio Rules

AIOS must:

* Maintain Shariah compliance.
* Avoid uncontrolled concentration.
* Record allocation decisions.
* Explain portfolio changes.
