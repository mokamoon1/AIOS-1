# 1. Purpose

This document defines how AIOS evaluates companies using fundamental analysis.

The objective is to understand business quality, financial strength, and long-term investment potential.

---

# 2. Fundamental Analysis Philosophy

AIOS evaluates companies based on business fundamentals, not price movement alone.

A good investment opportunity requires understanding:

* What the company does.
* How it generates revenue.
* Its financial health.
* Its competitive position.
* Its future potential.

---

# 3. Fundamental Analysis Position

Fundamental analysis is performed after:

```text
Shariah Verification

        ↓

Market Eligibility

        ↓

Company Evaluation
```

---

# 4. Company Analysis Framework

AIOS evaluates companies through five main areas:

```text
Business Quality

Financial Strength

Growth

Valuation

Risk
```

---

# 5. Business Quality Analysis

The system evaluates:

## Business Model

Questions:

* What products or services does the company provide?
* Is the business understandable?
* Is the business sustainable?

---

## Competitive Advantage

Evaluates:

* Market position.
* Brand strength.
* Technology advantage.
* Customer relationships.

---

## Management Quality

Evaluates:

* Leadership history.
* Strategic decisions.
* Corporate governance.

---

# 6. Financial Analysis

AIOS analyzes:

## Revenue

Measures:

* Business size.
* Growth consistency.

---

## Profitability

Metrics:

* Net profit.
* Operating margin.
* Return on equity.

---

## Balance Sheet

Evaluates:

* Assets.
* Liabilities.
* Financial stability.

---

## Cash Flow

Analyzes:

* Operating cash flow.
* Cash generation ability.

---

# 7. Growth Analysis

The system evaluates:

* Revenue growth.
* Earnings growth.
* Market expansion.
* Future opportunities.

---

# 8. Valuation Analysis

AIOS evaluates whether the current price is reasonable.

Possible metrics:

* Price/Earnings Ratio.
* Price/Sales Ratio.
* Enterprise Value Metrics.
* Historical valuation comparison.

---

# 9. Fundamental Scoring

The Fundamental Engine produces:

```text
Company Quality Score

Financial Strength Score

Growth Score

Valuation Score

Overall Fundamental Score
```

---

# 10. Integration With Other Agents

Fundamental results are sent to:

## Market Analysis Agent

To combine:

* Company quality.
* Market condition.

---

## Risk Agent

To evaluate:

* Financial risks.
* Exposure.

---

## CIO Agent

To support final decision.

---

# 11. Fundamental Rules

AIOS must:

* Avoid decisions based on one metric.
* Consider multiple factors.
* Store analysis history.
* Explain conclusions.

---

# 12. Limitations

Fundamental analysis cannot guarantee future performance.

AIOS uses it as one component among several decision factors.

---
