# 1. Purpose

This document defines the investment concepts, principles, and processes understood by AIOS.

It establishes the foundation for how the system views markets, assets, and investment decisions.

---

# 2. AIOS Investment Philosophy

AIOS is an intelligent investment analysis system designed to support disciplined, evidence-based investment decisions.

The system prioritizes:

* Capital preservation.
* Shariah compliance.
* Risk management.
* Long-term sustainability.
* Explainable decisions.

---

# 3. Investment Universe

The investment universe represents all assets available for evaluation.

AIOS V1 focuses on:

* Shariah-compliant stocks.
* Publicly traded companies.
* Selected market data sources.

Non-approved assets are excluded.

---

# 4. Investment Workflow

The investment process follows:

```text id="b7k4uz"
Shariah Approved Universe

        ↓

Market Data Collection

        ↓

Company Analysis

        ↓

Technical Analysis

        ↓

Risk Evaluation

        ↓

Portfolio Evaluation

        ↓

Investment Decision
```

---

# 5. Asset Understanding

AIOS recognizes financial assets as instruments with:

* Market price.
* Business value.
* Risk profile.
* Liquidity.
* Historical behavior.

---

# 6. Investment Decision Principles

AIOS decisions must consider:

## Opportunity

Questions:

* Is the asset attractive?
* Is there a valid investment thesis?

---

## Risk

Questions:

* What can go wrong?
* How much capital is exposed?

---

## Timing

Questions:

* Is the entry suitable?
* Is market condition supportive?

---

## Portfolio Impact

Questions:

* Does it improve diversification?
* Does it create excessive exposure?

---

# 7. Investment Horizon

AIOS V1 focuses on:

* Swing investment.
* Medium-term investment.
* Long-term investment analysis.

Excluded:

* High-frequency trading.
* Ultra-short speculation.

---

# 8. Investment Research Process

The system evaluates:

## Company

* Business quality.
* Financial condition.
* Sector position.

---

## Market

* Trend.
* Momentum.
* Market structure.

---

## Risk

* Volatility.
* Exposure.
* Potential loss.

---

# 9. Decision Output

AIOS does not produce unexplained actions.

Every recommendation must include:

* Asset.
* Reason.
* Supporting data.
* Risk level.
* Confidence score.

---

# 10. Domain Rules

AIOS must:

* Never analyze excluded assets.
* Never ignore risk.
* Never bypass Shariah verification.
* Never force a trade.
