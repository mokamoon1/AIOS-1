# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official acronyms used throughout AIOS.

The objective is to establish a single authoritative reference for abbreviations appearing in architecture documents, source code, technical documentation, operational procedures, governance records, and communications.

Whenever an acronym appears in AIOS documentation, its meaning shall conform to this document.

---

# 2. General Principles

Acronyms shall:

* Be used consistently.
* Have a single official meaning.
* Avoid ambiguity.
* Follow industry standards whenever practical.
* Be documented before official adoption.

# 12. Acronym Governance

Before introducing a new acronym:

* Verify it does not conflict with existing entries.
* Provide an official definition.
* Update this document.
* Reference the updated version in related documentation.

Unapproved acronyms shall not be used in official AIOS documentation.
