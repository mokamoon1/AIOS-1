# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official Maintenance Policy for AIOS.

The Maintenance Policy establishes the standards, procedures, and responsibilities required to keep AIOS reliable, secure, efficient, and aligned with its long-term architectural vision.

Maintenance is a continuous engineering process throughout the entire system lifecycle.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Maintenance Policy shall:

* Preserve software quality.
* Improve long-term maintainability.
* Reduce technical debt.
* Maintain security.
* Ensure operational continuity.
* Support continuous improvement.

---

# 3. Maintenance Principles

AIOS maintenance shall follow these principles:

* Prevent before repair.
* Automate whenever practical.
* Preserve architectural integrity.
* Maintain full traceability.
* Validate every change.
* Update documentation continuously.

Every maintenance activity shall be documented.

---

# 4. Maintenance Categories

Maintenance activities include:

* Preventive Maintenance.
* Corrective Maintenance.
* Adaptive Maintenance.
* Perfective Maintenance.
* Emergency Maintenance.

Each category shall follow documented procedures.

---

# 5. Preventive Maintenance

Preventive maintenance includes:

* Reviewing system health.
* Updating dependencies.
* Monitoring performance.
* Reviewing logs.
* Verifying backups.
* Testing recovery procedures.

Preventive activities reduce future operational risks.

---

# 6. Corrective Maintenance

Corrective maintenance addresses:

* Software defects.
* Configuration issues.
* Performance problems.
* Integration failures.
* Documentation corrections.

Every correction shall be verified through testing.

---

# 7. Adaptive Maintenance

Adaptive maintenance supports:

* New providers.
* New brokers.
* Infrastructure changes.
* Operating system updates.
* Regulatory changes.
* Future project requirements.

Adaptation shall preserve architectural consistency.

---

# 8. Perfective Maintenance

Perfective maintenance focuses on:

* Performance optimization.
* Code refactoring.
* Documentation improvement.
* Usability enhancement.
* Monitoring improvements.

Improvements shall not introduce regressions.

---

# 9. Emergency Maintenance

Emergency maintenance applies when:

* Critical failures occur.
* Security vulnerabilities are discovered.
* Production services become unavailable.
* Data integrity is threatened.

Emergency changes shall be documented and reviewed after implementation.

---

# 11. Documentation Maintenance

Maintenance shall include updates to:

* Architecture documents.
* Design documents.
* Requirements.
* Operational guides.
* Configuration documentation.
* Change history.

Documentation shall always reflect the current implementation.

---

# 12. Quality Assurance

Every maintenance activity shall verify:

* Functional correctness.
* Security compliance.
* Performance stability.
* Documentation accuracy.
* Regression testing results.

Quality assurance remains mandatory during maintenance.

---

# 13. Continuous Improvement

AIOS shall improve continuously through:

* Performance reviews.
* Incident analysis.
* Operational metrics.
* User feedback.
* Architectural reviews.

Lessons learned shall be incorporated into future improvements.

---

# 14. Future Expansion

Future maintenance capabilities may include:

* Predictive maintenance.
* AI-assisted diagnostics.
* Automated dependency management.
* Continuous architecture validation.
* Self-healing operational services.

The maintenance framework shall evolve alongside AIOS.

---

# 15. Success Criteria

The Maintenance Policy is considered successful when:

* System quality remains high.
* Technical debt remains controlled.
* Security posture remains strong.
* Documentation stays current.
* Operational stability is preserved.
