# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Audit Policy for AIOS.

The Audit Policy establishes the principles, responsibilities, procedures, evidence requirements, and reporting mechanisms necessary to verify that AIOS continuously complies with its architecture, governance policies, security requirements, operational procedures, and documented standards.

Auditing is an independent verification activity and shall remain objective.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Audit Policy shall:

* Verify governance compliance.
* Confirm policy adherence.
* Detect deviations.
* Improve operational quality.
* Support accountability.
* Preserve organizational knowledge.

---

# 3. Audit Principles

All audits shall follow these principles:

* Independence.
* Objectivity.
* Evidence-based evaluation.
* Repeatability.
* Transparency.
* Traceability.

Audit conclusions shall rely on documented evidence.

---

# 4. Audit Scope

Audits may evaluate:

* Project governance.
* Architecture.
* Software implementation.
* Security controls.
* Documentation.
* Testing activities.
* Deployment processes.
* Operational procedures.
* Compliance status.
* AI model governance.

Every critical domain shall remain auditable.

---

# 5. Audit Categories

AIOS supports:

* Governance Audit.
* Technical Audit.
* Security Audit.
* Compliance Audit.
* Documentation Audit.
* Operational Audit.
* Release Audit.

Each audit type follows documented procedures.

---

# 6. Audit Planning

Each audit shall define:

* Audit identifier.
* Scope.
* Objectives.
* Audit period.
* Responsible auditor.
* Evaluation criteria.

Audit planning shall be documented before execution.

---

# 7. Audit Evidence

Audit evidence may include:

* Policies.
* Architecture documents.
* Test reports.
* Deployment records.
* Change history.
* Risk Register.
* Configuration records.
* Operational logs.
* ADRs.

Evidence shall be authentic and verifiable.

---

---

# 10. Corrective Actions

Every audit finding shall include:

* Root cause.
* Corrective action.
* Responsible owner.
* Target completion date.
* Verification status.

Corrective actions shall remain traceable until closure.

---

# 11. Audit Reporting

Audit reports shall include:

* Audit identifier.
* Scope.
* Summary.
* Findings.
* Evidence.
* Recommendations.
* Corrective actions.
* Final conclusion.

Reports shall be archived permanently.

---

# 12. Periodic Audits

Periodic audits shall evaluate:

* Governance maturity.
* Documentation quality.
* Operational stability.
* Security posture.
* Compliance status.
* Continuous improvement progress.

Audit schedules shall follow governance policy.

---

# 13. Future Expansion

Future audit capabilities may include:

* Continuous auditing.
* AI-assisted audit analysis.
* Automated evidence collection.
* Continuous compliance dashboards.
* Predictive audit planning.

The audit framework shall evolve with AIOS.

---

# 14. Success Criteria

The Audit Policy is considered successful when:

* Policies are independently verified.
* Findings are evidence-based.
* Corrective actions are completed.
* Governance quality improves continuously.
* Audit history remains fully traceable.
