# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Risk Management framework for AIOS.

The Risk Management framework establishes the policies, processes, responsibilities, and controls required to identify, assess, prioritize, mitigate, monitor, and review risks throughout the AIOS lifecycle.

Risk management shall be integrated into every engineering and operational activity.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Risk Management framework shall:

* Identify risks early.
* Reduce operational uncertainty.
* Protect project objectives.
* Improve decision quality.
* Preserve business continuity.
* Support long-term sustainability.

---

# 3. Risk Management Principles

Risk management shall follow these principles:

* Continuous identification.
* Evidence-based assessment.
* Proactive mitigation.
* Continuous monitoring.
* Documented decision-making.
* Continuous improvement.

Risk awareness shall become part of daily operations.

---

# 5. Risk Categories

AIOS recognizes the following risk categories:

* Strategic Risks.
* Technical Risks.
* Operational Risks.
* Security Risks.
* Financial Risks.
* Compliance Risks.
* Infrastructure Risks.
* Data Risks.
* AI Model Risks.
* Third-Party Risks.

Each category shall have appropriate mitigation strategies.

---

# 6. Risk Identification

Risk identification shall consider:

* Architecture.
* Software quality.
* Infrastructure.
* External providers.
* Trading operations.
* Security.
* Documentation.
* Regulatory changes.

New risks shall be documented immediately after discovery.

---

# 7. Risk Assessment

Every identified risk shall evaluate:

* Probability.
* Impact.
* Detectability.
* Recovery complexity.
* Business consequences.

Risk evaluation shall be evidence-based.

---

# 8. Risk Classification

Risk levels shall be classified as:

```text id="v2rh5a"
Low

Medium

High

Critical
```

Classification determines mitigation priority.

---

# 9. Risk Response Strategies

Approved response strategies include:

* Risk Avoidance.
* Risk Reduction.
* Risk Transfer.
* Risk Acceptance.

Every significant risk shall have a documented response strategy.

---

# 10. Risk Register

The Risk Register shall record:

* Risk Identifier.
* Description.
* Category.
* Probability.
* Impact.
* Severity.
* Mitigation Plan.
* Risk Owner.
* Current Status.
* Review Date.

The Risk Register shall remain continuously updated.

---

# 11. Risk Monitoring

Operational monitoring shall continuously evaluate:

* System health.
* Security alerts.
* Performance degradation.
* Provider reliability.
* Infrastructure stability.
* Data integrity.

Monitoring results shall feed the Risk Register.

---

# 12. Risk Reviews

Periodic reviews shall verify:

* New risks.
* Closed risks.
* Mitigation effectiveness.
* Residual risk.
* Emerging threats.

Risk reviews shall occur according to governance policy.

---

# 13. Future Expansion

Future Risk Management capabilities may include:

* AI-assisted risk prediction.
* Automated risk scoring.
* Continuous compliance monitoring.
* Predictive infrastructure analysis.
* Dynamic risk dashboards.

The risk framework shall evolve with AIOS.

---

# 14. Success Criteria

The Risk Management framework is considered successful when:

* Risks are identified early.
* High-risk issues receive priority.
* Mitigation plans are effective.
* Operational disruptions are minimized.
* Governance decisions remain risk-informed.
